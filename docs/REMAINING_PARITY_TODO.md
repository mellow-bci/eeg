Vendor account/license/subscription enforcement is not a product feature to reproduce. Our own capabilities can operate without tiers, while resource bounds remain explicit and configurable where scientifically and operationally appropriate. No vendor binary, credential or proprietary trained asset is bundled. No vendor model assets are needed for our arithmetic/storage; exact mental-command comparison still needs a locally retained participant-trained Cortex profile and corresponding outputs. SQ tracing now identifies another local-only reference dependency: per-channel feature selections, Gaussian means/covariances and score thresholds, plus the active filter/window/band/SMQ recipe. Those assets have not been inspected or copied. Required assets for untraced gesture/performance models remain unknown and must be reported.
